# Concept Extraction

## Purpose

Transform a conversation into structured concepts.

---

# Pipeline

```text
Conversation

↓

Experience Extraction

↓

Concept Extraction

↓

Memory Creation
```

---

# Concept Types

## Entities

Specific concepts identified directly in the text.

Examples:

- Python
- Neural Networks
- ChromaDB

---

## Topics

Broader knowledge domains.

Examples:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Deep Learning
- Neuroscience

---

# Extraction Rules

## Prioritize relevance.

Only meaningful concepts should be extracted.

---

## Preserve context.

Concepts must remain connected to the original experience.

---

## Ignore conversational noise.

Do not store greetings, filler words, or unrelated information.
